## GLOBAL ANIMATION PRINCIPLES

### Transition Styles
- **Between beats:** Soft cross-dissolve (0.4s) — never harsh cuts
- **Text in:** Bounce scale (0 → 1.1 → 1.0) or slide-from-direction (0.3s ease-out)
- **Text out:** Fade-scale down (0.25s) or gentle wipe
- **Icons in:** Float up from below + fade in (0.5s ease-out)
- **Section changes:** Soft bloom/glow wipe — warm, feminine, non-clinical

### Icon Animation Style
- All icons: **outline style first**, then fill floods in (0.5s)
- Pad icon: gentle float bob (sinusoidal, 2s cycle)
- Calendar: pages flip softly (1 per second when active)
- Drop/period icon: soft pulse scale (breathes in-out, 2s cycle)
- Hibiscus/flower: petals animate open (radial unfurl, 1s)
- Heart: draws itself in thin stroke, then fills soft pink
- Silhouettes: walk-in animation, then settle + breathe (subtle scale pulse)

### Text Hierarchy Rules
- **Hero words** (DIGNITY, CONFIDENCE, SUPPORT, CARE): Deep rose or lilac, Playfair Display Bold, large
- **Body phrases**: Nude brown, DM Sans Medium, medium
- **Labels/captions**: Soft pink, Nunito SemiBold, small
- Never more than 2 lines visible simultaneously
- Text always lives in upper third OR lower third — never covers Dr. P's face

### Camera / Zoom Progression
```
0:00  1.00x  (wide, welcoming)
0:03  1.03x  (subtle draw in)
```

## NOTES FOR EDITOR

1. **Dr. P's avatar is the anchor** — all graphics serve her, never compete with her face or obstruct her mouth/eyes
2. **Left side and upper zone** are the primary motion graphic real estate (Dr. P sits slightly right-center in her existing setup)
3. Keep all animated elements **above 200px from bottom edge** (platform UI safe zone)
4. Use **easing curves** (ease-in-out) on all animations — no linear motion
5. The silhouettes should feel **hand-drawn / sketch aesthetic** — not clipart; use thin linework at 60–80% opacity
6. **Dignity moment (0:38–0:48)** should be the visual peak — most graphic density, warmest glow, most emotional
7. Test on both **dark mode and light mode** previews before export
8. Add **captions/subtitles** in CapCut auto-caption using brand font — this video will be watched without sound by many users
